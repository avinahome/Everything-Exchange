# APK Installation Guide for Everything Exchange

## For Android Users (Novice-Friendly)

### Step 1: Download the APK
1. Download the `app-debug.apk` file to your Android device
2. You can do this by:
   - Email the APK file to yourself and download it on your phone
   - Transfer via USB cable to your device
   - Use a cloud storage service (Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.)

### Step 2: Enable Installation from Unknown Sources
1. Open **Settings** on your Android device
2. Look for **Security** or **Privacy** (location varies by Android version)
3. Find **Install unknown apps** or **Unknown sources**
4. Select your file manager or browser
5. Toggle **Allow from this source** to ON

### Step 3: Install the App
1. Open your **File Manager** or **Downloads** app
2. Navigate to where you saved the APK file
3. Tap on the **app-debug.apk** file
4. Tap **Install** when prompted
5. Wait for installation to complete
6. Tap **Open** to launch the app

### Step 4: First-Time Setup
1. When you first open the app, you'll be on the **Profile** tab
2. Create your account by entering:
   - Username (choose any name you like)
   - Email address
   - Your location (city/area)
3. Tap **Create Account**

### Step 5: Start Using the App
1. **Inventory Tab**: Add your household items here
   - Tap the **+** button to add new items
   - Enter item name, description, category, and optional price
   - Use **Export CSV** or **Export PDF** to backup your inventory

2. **Discover Tab**: Browse mock items for sale
   - See sample items with prices and distances
   - Practice making offers

3. **Offers Tab**: View your offers
   - **Received**: Offers others made on your items
   - **Sent**: Offers you made on other items

4. **Listings Tab**: Manage items you want to sell
   - Future feature for creating sales listings

## Troubleshooting

### "Install Blocked" Error
- Make sure you enabled "Unknown sources" in Security settings
- Try installing from a different file manager app

### App Won't Open
- Restart your device and try again
- Make sure your Android version is 7.0 (API 24) or higher

### Features Not Working
- This is a demo/MVP version with some mock features
- The app works entirely offline - no internet required
- All data is stored locally on your device

## System Requirements
- **Android Version**: 7.0 (Nougat) or higher
- **Storage**: Approximately 50MB for the app
- **RAM**: 2GB+ recommended
- **Permissions**: Camera (for future photo features), Storage (for exports)

## Data Privacy
- All your data stays on your device
- No internet connection required
- No data is sent to external servers
- Your inventory and personal information remain private

## Need Help?
This app is designed to be intuitive, but if you need assistance:
1. Check that you followed all installation steps
2. Restart the app if it seems unresponsive
3. Clear the app's data in Android Settings > Apps if needed
